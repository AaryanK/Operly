The capability layer

Give AI more than a chat box. Give it capabilities.

OPERLY puts context, tools, business services and software projects behind one operating layer. The model can discover what is available, use what it is authorized to use and stay inside the boundaries of the current person or workspace.

Explicit authority

Powerful doesn't mean unrestricted.

OPERLY separates what an AI can understand from what it is allowed to do. Capabilities remain scoped to the current identity and workspace, with the capability firewall and approval lifecycle sitting between reasoning and consequential execution.

IDENTITY

Scoped context

Personal AI, workspace identity and connected channels keep authority attached to the right person and operating context.

FIREWALL

Authorized capabilities

Discovering that a capability exists is not the same thing as receiving permission to execute it.

APPROVAL

Human control when needed

Consequential operations can enter explicit approval and verification flows rather than silently executing.

PROVENANCE

Actions remain visible

Operational activity and provenance are part of the system instead of disappearing into an opaque agent conversation.
